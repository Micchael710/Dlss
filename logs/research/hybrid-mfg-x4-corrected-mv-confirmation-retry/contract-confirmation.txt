CONTRACT_CONFIRMATION_SPECIFICATION

EXPERIMENT:
HYBRID_X4_CORRECTED_MV_CONFIRMATION_RETRY

CORRECTED DUAL-CONTRACT DESIGN:
1. CUSTOM INTERPOLATOR (G25 / G75):
   - MV Buffer Unit: NDC_CLIP_SPACE [-1, 1]
   - Coordinates: mvecNDC = (2 * dx / Width, -2 * dy / Height)
   - Direction: current_to_previous
   - Shader Algorithm: Restored 100% byte/functionally identical to hybrid-mfg-x4-depth-contract.
   - Resource: CustomMotionA, CustomMotionB (separate DXGI_FORMAT_R32G32_FLOAT textures).

2. NVIDIA DLSS FG (G50):
   - MV Buffer Unit: SCREEN_SPACE_PIXELS
   - Coordinates: motionPixels = (dx, dy)
   - Direction: current_to_previous
   - Scale: mvecScaleX = 1.0, mvecScaleY = 1.0
   - cameraMotionIncluded: true
   - Resource: NvidiaMotionA, NvidiaMotionB (separate DXGI_FORMAT_R32G32_FLOAT textures).

PHYSICAL MOTION DISPLACEMENTS:
- Background: (-8, -4) px
- Midground:  (-16, -12) px
- Foreground: (-28, +8) px

SEPARATION:
CUSTOM_NVIDIA_MV_RESOURCES_SEPARATE=YES
Neither algorithm is forced to consume an incompatible contract.
