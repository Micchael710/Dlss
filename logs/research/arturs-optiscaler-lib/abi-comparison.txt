ANALYSIS OF MEMORY ACCESS AND ALIGNMENT:
1. In dlss-enabler-main, OptiScalerConfig is allocated with 2 bytes. The caller sets:
     cfg.spoof_as_enabler = true (offset 0)
     cfg.isFrs4UpdateOff = false (offset 1)
2. In dlss-enabler-optiscaler-embedder (commit 56f10d2), OptiScaler_Init accesses:
     const bool spoof = cfg && cfg->spoof_as_enabler;
   This reads offset 0. Since the caller allocated at least 2 bytes, reading 1 byte at offset 0 is memory-safe (no out-of-bounds read).
3. However, semantic divergence exists:
   dlss-enabler-optiscaler-embedder (Feb 1, 2026) predates the addition of isFrs4UpdateOff in dlss-enabler-main (Sep 2026). The embedder does not recognize or handle isFrs4UpdateOff.
