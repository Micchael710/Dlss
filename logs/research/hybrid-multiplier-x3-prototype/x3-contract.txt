HYBRID MULTIPLIER X3 CONTRACT
==============================
EXPERIMENT: HYBRID_MULTIPLIER_X3_PROTOTYPE

1. SEQUENCE AND PRESENTATION CONTRACT:
   Presented sequence:
     REAL A (t = 0.000000)
     G33    (t = 1/3 = 0.3333333333)
     G67    (t = 2/3 = 0.6666666667)
     REAL B (t = 1.000000)
   
   Presented generated frame count: 2
   Internal diagnostic frame count: 1 (NVIDIA G50)
   Multiplier: x3 (2 generated frames per real interval)
   Intervals: [1/3, 1/3, 1/3]
   Equal interval contract: VALID

2. MOTION VECTOR CONTRACTS:
   Custom interpolator: NDC clip-space [-1, 1], current_to_previous
   NVIDIA DLSS FG: SCREEN_SPACE_PIXELS, current_to_previous, mvecScale=(1.0, 1.0)
   Separate resources: YES

3. ROLE OF NVIDIA G50:
   NVIDIA_G50_ROLE = DIAGNOSTIC_INTERNAL_ANCHOR
   NVIDIA_G50_USED_AS_X3_OUTPUT = NO
   NVIDIA_G50_USED_AS_GENERATION_INPUT_FOR_G33 = NO
   NVIDIA_G50_USED_AS_GENERATION_INPUT_FOR_G67 = NO
   X3_NVIDIA_DIRECT_CONTRIBUTION = NO

4. GROUND TRUTH POLICY:
   REF33, REF67, REF50, D33, D67, D50 are generated EXCLUSIVELY for post-generation validation.
   GROUND_TRUTH_USED_AS_GENERATION_INPUT = NO
   Any ground truth leakage into generation inputs constitutes FAIL_STAGE = GROUND_TRUTH_LEAKAGE.

5. QUALITY AND POSITION CRITERIA:
   OVERALL_RGB_MAE <= 0.08
   OVERALL_BAD64_FRACTION <= 0.10
   CHALLENGING_RGB_MAE <= 0.18
   CHALLENGING_BAD64_FRACTION <= 0.25
   POSITION_ERROR_LIMIT_PIXELS <= 2.0 px
